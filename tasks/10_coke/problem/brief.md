#Coke blending demand

We are responsible for the coal blending arrangements for coking production. We hope that you will provide an executable coal blending plan within the planning period based on the existing coal inventory, price and coal quality. On the premise of meeting production and quality requirements, we hope that the raw material cost per ton of coal blending will be as low as possible.

Please provide the corresponding coal blending proportion plan for each piece of data. We will provide the required data; you can ask us about the ratio requirements, quality acceptance criteria and delivery format.
