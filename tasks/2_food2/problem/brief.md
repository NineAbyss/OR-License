# The day’s production schedule for the stewed pot team

Our team has multiple stew pots running at the same time, and has to complete the production tasks of multiple batches and different products every day.
The brine state of each pot is different when it is started. After using it for several production cycles, the brine needs to be replaced. The product arrangement and
The timing of brine replacement should be considered together.

Please arrange a production plan for us that meets the process requirements based on the day's demand and equipment conditions.
The quantity of each batch and each product must be completed according to demand, neither more nor less. We hope that the team will complete it as soon as possible
During the day's production, important products should be arranged as far forward as possible.

Which batch and when to change the brine is convenient for the team to organize production accordingly.
