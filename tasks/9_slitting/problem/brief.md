# Material allocation requirements

Our factory has a batch of orders for slitting materials, and we need to process the wide parent rolls into the specifications required by the customer according to the existing slitting rules. We have some of our own inventory and can also outsource master rolls. This time we only arrange this batch of orders. We hope to buy as little external materials as possible on the premise that all the orders are delivered and the inventory is not overused.

We provide order requirements, slitting rules and inventory data. Please give the material allocation plan, explain what rules are used for each line, how many mother rolls are invested, which strips and quantities are delivered, how much margin is left, and distinguish between own and outsourced sources.

Plans are delivered individually on a per-instance basis. If the quantity unit, content format, cutting requirements and acceptance criteria are unclear, you can ask us.
